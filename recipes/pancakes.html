Classic Pancakes

Description

Pancakes are a classic breakfast dish made from a simple batter that creates a light, fluffy, and tender texture. They have a slightly sweet flavor and a golden-brown surface, making them a comforting and satisfying meal to enjoy any time of day.

They are traditionally served warm with butter and maple syrup, but can also be topped with fresh fruit, whipped cream, powdered sugar, or chocolate chips. Their simple ingredients and quick preparation make pancakes an easy and delicious homemade breakfast.

Ingredients

For about 8 pancakes:

  • All-purpose flour - 190 g
  • Baking powder - 8 g
  • Granulated sugar - 25 g
  • Salt - 2 g
  • Milk - 240 ml
  • Egg - 1 large
  • Unsalted butter, melted - 30 g
  • Vanilla extract - 5 ml
  • Butter or oil for cooking - 15 ml

Steps

  1. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, sugar and salt.
  2. In a separate bowl, whisk together the milk, egg, melted butter, and vanilla extract.
  3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and gently mix until combined. Do no overmix; a few small lumps are fine.
  4. Heat a lightly greased pan or skillet over medium heat.
  5. Pour about 60 ml of batter (a small expresso cup) onto the pan for each pancake.
  6. Cook until bubbles begin to form on the surface and the edge looks set, about 2 - 3 minutes.
  7. Flip the pancake and cook for another 1 - 2 minutes, until golden brown.
  8. Repeat with the remaining batter, adding a little more butter or oil to the pan as needed.
  9. Serve the pancakes warm with your favorite toppings.
