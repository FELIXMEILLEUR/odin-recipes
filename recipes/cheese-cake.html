Cheese Cake

Description

Cheesecake is a rich and creamy dessert made with a smooth filling of cream cheese, sugar, and eggs on top of a buttery crust. The filling has a soft, velvety texture with a slightly tangy flavor that balances the sweetness. Depending on the recipe, cheesecake can be flavored with vanilla, lemon, chocolate, or fresh fruit.

A classic cheesecake is baked until the edges are set while the center remains slightly soft and creamy. Once cooled and chilled, it can be served plain or topped with fresh berries, fruit sauce, whipped cream, or other toppings. Its combination of a crisp, buttery crust and rich, creamy filling makes it a popular dessert for almost any occasion.

Ingredients

For a classic baked cheesecake, about 8 servings:

  • Cream cheese - 680 g
  • Granulated sugar - 150 g
  • Eggs - 3 large
  • Sour cream - 120 g
  • Vanilla extract - 5 ml
  • All-purpose flour - 15 g
  • Graham cracker crumbs - 200 g
  • Unsalted butter - 80 g
  • Salt - 2 g
  • Lemon juice - 15 ml

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 175°C.
  2. Crush the graham crackers into fine crumbs and mix them with melted butter.
  3. Press the mixture firmly into the bottom of a springform pan to create the crust.
  4. Bake the crust for about 8 - 10 minutes, then let it cool slighty.
  5. Beat the cream cheese and sugar together until smooth and creamy.
  6. Add the eggs one at a time, mixing gently after each addition.
  7. Add the sour cream, vanilla extract, flour, salt, and lemon juice. Mix until combined.
  8. Pour the cheesecake filling over the cooled crust and smooth the top.
  9. Bake for approximately 50 - 60 minutes, until the edges are set but the center still has a slight jiggle.
  10. Turn off the oven, crack the oven door open, and let the cheesecake cool inside for about 1 hour.
  11. Remove it from the oven and let it cool completely at room temperature.
  12. Refrigerate for at least 4 hours, preferably overnight, before slicing and serving.
